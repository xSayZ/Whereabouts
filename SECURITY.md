# Security and privacy

Whereabouts sends your map ID and two position numbers to your own guild, only while sharing is on.
It sends nothing to anyone else and has no network access of its own.

## Report a problem
- Use "Report a vulnerability" on the repository's Security tab (private).
- Examples worth reporting: a way to make the addon send to someone outside the guild, to send while sharing is
  off, or to reveal a position to a non-guildmate.
- Not fixable here: a guildmate running a modified client can log positions of players who have sharing on.
